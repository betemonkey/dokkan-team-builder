Mockup: pick categories like in the game

“＋ Add a category” opens a popup with all 99 categories, the ones with your units first. Tick one or more, choose Any of them (OR) or All of them (AND), then OK: the board only uses units that match. Real data: unit counts from the category pages of dokkanbattle.net (LR and UR), leaders and units worked out for your box (Gomah, Broly (Wrathful), LSSJ Broly, SSJ3 Goku).

The popup, and the board after OK

Left: the popup open on the Fold cover, with Pure Saiyans and Movie Bosses ticked in AND: only the units in both count (46). Middle: the same after OK. Right: an OR example on PC (Full Power or Pure Saiyans: 802 units), where no leader boosts all of them, so the units it misses get a ⚠. Everything is clickable: tick categories, switch OR / AND, search, OK, ✕ on a chip, the “All of / Any of” chip, a leader, an empty slot, Put in.
