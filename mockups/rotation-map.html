Dokkan Team Builder: rotation map mockups

Static mockups using your real Festival team (Gomah + Broly (Wrathful) + LSSJ Broly, Angel Goku & Vegeta leading). Link counts are real; the ⚔/🛡 link-bonus numbers are worked out from link level 1 and are illustrative. In both options the tool would also arrange the rotation itself, putting the best-linked units next to each other.

Option A: Rotation map (DokkanStats-style)

Leader & friend up top with the team's total leader boost. Below, the rotation: two rotation pairs on the left/middle, floaters on the right. One line per shared link, one colour per pair, so you see at a glance who links with whom. Each unit shows its leader (L) / friend (F) boost and the ATK/DEF it gets from active links.
