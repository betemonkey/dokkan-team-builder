Mockups: Teams that cleared Festival of Battles

Your real data: your 3 picks and your 13-unit box. Pick A, B or C (or mix: "A, but with the chips of B").

What's wrong in the current version
  • The "L" and "F" labels sit above the leader and friend, so those two cards are lower than the five subs.
  • The ⓘ buttons cover the type badges in the top-right corner of every card.
  • Names wrap to 1–4 lines, so the YOUR PICK / OWNED tags end up at different heights.
  • Missing units aren't marked, and each team is about 300 px tall: three teams fill the screen.

A · One line-up per team

Seven cards on one aligned row: leader, the five subs, friend, split by thin lines. The L/F badge sits on the card's free top-left corner, so every card starts at the same height. Names are two lines at most and the tags share one row. No ⓘ: tap a card for its details. On the phone the names hide and the tags become coloured dots.

B · Leader and friend, then the subs

Same layout as your team board: LEADER and FRIEND on the left, the five subs on the right, with one-word chips on top (your picks, what you own, what's missing). On the phone, leader and friend go on top and the subs below.

C · Compact list, tap to open

Each team is one line: seven small cards (a gold or green underline for your picks and owned units) and a one-line summary. Tap a line to open it into option A's view. All five teams fit on one screen.
