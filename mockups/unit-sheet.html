Dokkan Team Builder: unit details mockups

Static mockups with real data for Broly (Wrathful) in your Festival team. Either option opens for any unit: on the map, in search results, in "Also fits", in My box.

Option A: Unit sheet (slides in)

Tap any unit and a sheet slides in: from the right on PC and the unfolded Fold, from the bottom on the phone. The two actions sit right under the name, big and always visible: I don't have it (red) and Swap…. Below, three tabs keep the rest tidy: Details (leader skill, super attack, passive with the stacking lines marked, categories, links at max level), In this team (who it links with), Swap (the swap-in grid). On PC, hovering a unit also shows a small preview without clicking.

Option B: Info card under the map + hover preview

No overlay: tapping a unit fills a two-column card under the map. Left: the unit and its actions as big buttons. Right: folding sections (Links in this team · Swap in · Full details), one open at a time. Hovering any unit (PC) pops a compact preview with the leader skill, super attack and stacking lines. Simpler, but on the phone the card is below the map, so you scroll down to it.
