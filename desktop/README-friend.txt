DOKKAN TEAM BUILDER
===================

Start: double-click "Dokkan Team Builder.exe" (keep it inside this folder,
next to the "app" folder).

First launch: Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app
isn't signed. Click "More info" then "Run anyway". It only asks once.

How to use
- My box: search the units you own and tap them to add them.
- Build a team: pick up to 6 units you want to use (search, or tap them in
  "From my box"), tick "Festival of Battles" if you want stackers, then
  press "Build team".
- Tap any unit on the team map for its details, to replace it, or to say
  you don't have it. The (i) on any card shows its details too.

Good to know
- Unit data comes from web-api.eu and dokkanbattle.net, card art from
  dokkaninfo. It's fetched when needed and kept for a week, so the first
  build of new units takes a little while.
- Your box and settings are saved in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\DokkanTeamBuilder (delete that folder to start fresh).
- Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
